Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Our Lawn Care Service, Answered

Straight answers about contracts, pricing, scheduling, and who actually shows up to do the work — no fine print, no surprises.

Do I need to sign a contract?
No long-term contract required. We work with you on a season-by-season or as-needed basis — cancel or pause anytime with a quick message.
How often will my lawn be cut?
Most customers choose weekly or bi-weekly mowing, depending on how fast your lawn grows and your preference. We'll recommend a schedule when we quote your property.
Do you offer one-time services, or only ongoing maintenance?
Both. Whether you need a single spring or fall cleanup, a one-time grass cut before listing your home, or year-round service, we're happy to help either way.
How much does lawn care cost?
Pricing depends on your property size, the services you need, and how often you'd like service. Message us your address and we'll send a free, no-obligation quote.
What if I'm not happy with a visit?
Just let us know and we'll make it right — that's our satisfaction guarantee. We're a small, owner-operated business, so your feedback goes directly to the person doing the work.
Who actually does the work — is it you, or a crew?
The owner is on every job, personally. No subcontractors, no rotating crews — the person who quotes your lawn is the same person who takes care of it.
What areas do you service?
We serve Brampton, Mississauga, Oakville, and Milton, Ontario.
How do I get started?
Call, message us on WhatsApp, or request a free quote online — whichever is easiest for you. We typically respond within 24 hours, often sooner.
