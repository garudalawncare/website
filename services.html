Grass Cutting & Lawn Mowing

Our weekly and bi-weekly grass cutting service keeps your lawn at the ideal height for healthy, dense growth. We mow with a properly maintained push mower set to the correct seasonal height, trim the edges, and blow off clippings from your driveway and walkway — leaving your property clean and tidy every visit.

  • Weekly or bi-weekly scheduling to match your lawn's growth rate
  • Correct cutting height for the season — never scalped, never left shaggy
  • Clippings cleared from hard surfaces every visit

Edge Trimming

A proper string trimmer creates the sharp, defined lines along driveways, sidewalks, and garden beds that make a lawn look genuinely finished. It's a small detail that makes a big visual difference — and one a lot of cheaper services skip.

Lawn Fertilization

Proper fertilization is the foundation of a thick, green lawn. We use a broadcast spreader to apply season-appropriate fertilizer evenly across your property — strengthening root systems, improving colour, and helping your grass naturally crowd out weeds instead of relying on heavy herbicide use.

Shrub & Hedge Trimming

Overgrown shrubs and hedges make even a perfectly mowed lawn look unkempt. Using proper pruning shears, we shape your hedges, remove dead growth, and keep your landscaping looking deliberate and well cared for throughout the growing season.

Sod Installation

Whether you need to repair bare patches or replace a full lawn, we handle sod supply and installation from start to finish — preparing the soil, laying the sod tight with no visible seams, and rolling and watering it properly so it roots well from day one.

Spring & Fall Cleanup

Seasonal cleanups protect your lawn before and after the harshest months. Our spring cleanup clears winter debris, dead leaves, and built-up thatch. Our fall cleanup rakes and removes fallen leaves before they smother your grass, preparing your lawn for a strong return in spring.
