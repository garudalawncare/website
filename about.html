About Us

Owner-Operated Lawn Care, Based in Brampton

Garuda Lawn Care is a locally-owned, owner-operated lawn care and landscaping company serving homeowners across Brampton, Mississauga, Oakville, and Milton.

Who We Are

Garuda Lawn Care was built on a simple idea: when you hire the owner, you get better work. We provide grass cutting, edge trimming, fertilization, hedge trimming, sod installation, and seasonal cleanup — and every job is done by the person who quoted it, not a rotating crew dispatched by a call centre.

What Makes Us Different

There's no shortage of lawn care companies in the GTA West. Here's why homeowners keep coming back to Garuda Lawn Care:

  • Owner on every job — the person who quoted your lawn is the same person cutting it.
  • Reliable scheduling — we show up on the agreed day, every time, and communicate immediately if anything changes.
  • Honest pricing — clear quotes with no hidden fees or mid-season surprises.
  • Satisfaction guarantee — if you're not happy, we come back and make it right.

Our Commitment to the Communities We Serve

We're proud to serve homeowners across Brampton, Mississauga, Oakville, and Milton. As a small, locally-operated business, our reputation is everything to us — and every lawn we maintain reflects that.
